Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Overview

NFC Coins is an Android application that interacts with NFC cards to store and read coin balance information.

Data Collection

The app may send anonymous usage statistics to a server run by the developer, such as:

  • Device manufacturer and model
  • App version
  • Country
  • Anonymous usage counters (e.g. how often a feature is used)

This data would be used only to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It is not designed to identify individual users, and it will never include your name, contacts, files, card balances, or secret keys, nor be sold or shared with third parties. If usage statistics are introduced, this policy will be updated first.

Data Stored on NFC Cards

The only information written to NFC cards is:

  • Coin balance
  • Birth year (used for age-based discount validation)

This data is stored exclusively on the physical NFC card in your possession. The developer has no access to it.

Device Permissions

The app requests access to the device's NFC hardware solely to read and write NFC cards. No other data from your device is accessed.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact the developer at the address listed on the app's Google Play Store page.
